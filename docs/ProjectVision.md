# SkillPilot AI

## 1. Vision

To become the most trusted AI-powered career platform that helps students learn the right skills, prepare for interviews, build professional resumes, and secure their dream jobs.

## 2. Problem Statement

Students preparing for software jobs struggle to find the right learning path, prepare effectively for interviews, create industry-standard resumes, and track job applications because they rely on multiple disconnected platforms. This leads to confusion, wasted time, inconsistent preparation, and missed job opportunities.

## 3. Target Users

Primary Users

- Final-year engineering students
- Fresh graduates
- Self-taught developers
- Job seekers preparing for software roles

Secondary Users

- Working professionals looking for career growth
- College placement cells
- Training institutes

## 4. Goals

- Help students follow a structured learning roadmap.
- Improve interview preparation through AI mock interviews.
- Enable users to build ATS-friendly resumes.
- Help users track job applications.
- Provide personalized career guidance.

## 5. Core Features

- AI Career Roadmap
- AI Mock Interview
- Resume Builder
- Portfolio Builder
- Coding Practice
- Job Tracker
- Dashboard
- Progress Analytics

## 6. Revenue Model

- Freemium model
- Monthly subscription
- Premium AI interview credits
- College partnerships
- Recruiter dashboard

## 7. Competitors

| Platform                 | Strength              | Weakness                    |
| ------------------------ | --------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Coding Practice Platform | Great coding problems | No career roadmap           |
| Resume Builder           | Easy resume creation  | No interview preparation    |
| Job Portal               | Huge job listings     | No personalized guidance    |
| Interview Platform       | Good mock interviews  | No complete career tracking |


## 8. Why Will Users Choose Us?

SkillPilot AI brings learning, interview preparation, resume building, portfolio creation, and job tracking into one platform with personalized AI guidance. Instead of using multiple disconnected tools, students get a complete career journey in a single place.
